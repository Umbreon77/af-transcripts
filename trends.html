Read with care
  • Counts come from the automatic captions (with the site's name corrections), so misheard words are missed or miscounted.
  • They include words he reads aloud or plays in clips, not only his own. Superchat segments are left out.
  • Months with fewer or shorter shows have lower counts; per hour of show evens that out. The first two months (Nov–Dec 2024) cover only 12 episodes.
  • The latest month is only part of a month until it ends.
  • Only words and two-word phrases said 20 times or more in total are kept.
  • Research's All mentions matches inside words too ("Iran" also finds "Iranian"), so its counts there can run higher.
