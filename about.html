What it is

A searchable archive of America First, the nightly show hosted by Nick Fuentes. It has a transcript of every episode it covers: regular episodes from January 2025 on, a dozen from late 2024, and some interviews and specials. Older shows are mostly not here yet. New episodes are added after they air.

Each episode's date is the day the stream started, New York time.

Where the text comes from

The transcripts are Rumble's automatic captions of each video; a handful of shows with no captions were transcribed by automatic speech-to-text instead. They are not corrected by hand. Expect misheard words and missing punctuation. Names the captions often get wrong are corrected by a list of reviewed spellings (for example "Peter Teal" is shown as Peter Thiel, and "Potteritz" as Podhoretz). A corrected name has a faint dotted underline; hover over it, or tap it, to see what the caption said. The original captions are kept unchanged, and names the list doesn't cover still appear as captioned.

Times match the video. Each transcript starts where the show proper begins (the pre-show isn't transcribed), so the first line can be hours into the stream. Every time opens the video on Rumble at that second. Captions can be a few seconds off, so you may need to rewind a little.

Chapters

Most episodes have chapter titles written by a viewer, @bakeddgoron, who posts a timestamped outline under each video on Rumble. They are shown as written, credited, and are not the site's own words. Episodes without them get auto chapters, generated from the summaries described below and labeled "Auto chapter".

Summaries and Nick's Positions

The short summaries of each part of a show (the results marked Summary · not a quote in Research, the auto chapters, and the episode blurbs) and the Nick's Positions pages were drafted with AI (Claude, made by Anthropic) from the transcripts. The site's owner reviewed every Nick's Positions page; the short summaries were spot-checked but not each reviewed, so a summary can be wrong. Check the transcript.

  • Summaries are descriptions, not quotes. Use them to find things; quote only his words from the transcript.
  • Nick's Positions sets out his views as he states them, attributed to him ("he argues"), without commentary. It covers the main show, not the paid questions read at the end.
  • Some positions also have key passages: longer stretches where he makes the whole argument, verbatim.
  • Clips, readings and guests are labeled where the index marks them ("per the index"), so someone else's words aren't taken for his. Those labels are generated too, and can occasionally be wrong.

How quotes are checked

Every quote and key passage in Nick's Positions, and every ❝ He said result in Research, is checked word for word against the transcript by a script before it is published, and links to the exact second it was said. The check confirms the words are in the captions (with the same name corrections as the transcript pages); it can't catch a caption that misheard him, so listen to the video before relying on a quote.

Independence

This site is not affiliated with Nick Fuentes, America First, or Rumble. It doesn't host the videos; it links to them on Rumble.

How to cite

Use Cite on a transcript line or a passage, a quote in Nick's Positions, or Research's preview (how). It gives a short citation like this; Full adds the episode's title and a video link.

"Why can the government remove the visas of 250,000 people, but they will not follow through on mass deportations? It's very simple and easy because antisemitism is seen as something that is absolutely intolerable. It's unacceptable under any circumstances, no matter what."
— Nick Fuentes, America First Ep. 1759, Oct 6, 2026 (4:01:54–4:02:10)
https://groyplax.github.io/af-transcripts/ep/1759/#t=14514-14530

Check before you quote. The text is automatic captions, so click the time and listen to the video. Links to an episode or a line are permanent and safe to share.
